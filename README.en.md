## Quick Selection Table

| Medium Characteristics | Recommended Pump | Main Reason |
|----------------------|------------------|-------------|
| Highly corrosive, flammable | Magnetic Pump | Static seal, no leakage |
| Precise metering | Metering Pump | High flow accuracy |
| Solids, high viscosity | Diaphragm Pump | Good throughput |
| Low viscosity, clean water | Centrifugal Pump | Versatile, high efficiency |

## Key Selection Parameters

- **Medium properties**: concentration, temperature, density, viscosity
- **Flow requirements**: maximum flow, normal flow
- **Head requirements**: actual head, friction loss
- **Material selection**: metal (stainless/alloy) or non-metal (PP/PVDF/ETFE)
- **Temperature range**: operating temperature, maximum temperature
- **Cleanliness requirements**: SEMI, pharmaceutical grade

## Selection Steps

1. Collect working condition parameters
2. Determine pump type category
3. Select product series
4. Confirm details (interfaces, motor, auxiliaries)
