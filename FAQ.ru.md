## Quick FAQ

### Q1: How to choose pump type?
- Corrosive/explosive → Magnetic Pump
- Precise metering → Metering Pump
- Solids/high viscosity → Diaphragm Pump
- Clean water → Centrifugal Pump

### Q2: Magnetic vs Centrifugal?
- **Magnetic**: Static seal, no leakage
- **Centrifugal**: Mechanical seal, slight leakage

### Q3: Diaphragm pump applications?
- Solids slurry
- High viscosity fluids
- Flammable/explosive media

### Q4: Metering pump accuracy?
- Standard: ±1%
- Precision: ±0.5%
- High precision: ±0.25%

### Q5: How to determine pump head?
Head = Actual head + Pipe friction + Valve losses + Safety margin (10-20%)

### Q6: Can magnetic pumps handle solids?
**Not recommended**. Clean media only.
